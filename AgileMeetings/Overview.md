# Agile Meetings: Sprint Overview

Which Scrum ceremony happens when during a sprint, and where to find details on how to run each one.

> Examples below assume a **2-week sprint** on a **Sunday–Thursday work week**. If your team runs 1-week sprints or a different work week, keep the same order and adjust the days/durations accordingly — the structure doesn't change, just the calendar.

## 2. Sprint Timeline

A 2-week (10 working day) sprint, laid out day by day:

```
Day 1  (Sun, wk1)  ── Sprint Planning (AM)  ──▶  Sprint begins
Day 1  (Sun, wk1)  ── Daily Standup
Day 2  (Mon, wk1)  ── Daily Standup
Day 3  (Tue, wk1)  ── Daily Standup
Day 4  (Wed, wk1)  ── Daily Standup
Day 5  (Thu, wk1)  ── Daily Standup
Day 6  (Sun, wk2)  ── Daily Standup  +  Backlog Refinement (mid-sprint)
Day 7  (Mon, wk2)  ── Daily Standup
Day 8  (Tue, wk2)  ── Daily Standup
Day 9  (Wed, wk2)  ── Daily Standup
Day 10 (Thu, wk2)  ── Daily Standup (brief)
Day 10 (Thu, wk2)  ── Sprint Review (AM)
Day 10 (Thu, wk2)  ── Sprint Retrospective (PM)  ──▶  Sprint ends
                       │
                       ▼
              Next Sprint Planning (Day 1 of next sprint)
```

Key ordering rules:
- **Sprint Planning** always opens the sprint; **Sprint Review** and **Sprint Retrospective** always close it, in that order (review the product first, then reflect on the process).
- **Backlog Refinement** happens *mid-sprint*, not right before planning — refined items need to be ready and estimated *before* planning starts, not decided in the moment.
- **Daily Standup** runs every working day of the sprint, including the days Planning/Review/Retro happen (keep it short those days, don't skip it).

## 3. Cadence Summary

| Meeting | Frequency | Typical duration (2-wk sprint) | Attendees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint Planning | Once per sprint, day 1 | ~4 hours | Whole Scrum team |
| Daily Standup | Every working day | 15 minutes | Dev team (PO/SM optional) |
| Backlog Refinement | 1–2x per sprint, mid-sprint | 1 hour | PO + dev team reps |
| Sprint Review | Once per sprint, last day | ~2 hours | Whole team + stakeholders |
| Sprint Retrospective | Once per sprint, last day, after Review | ~1.5 hours | Scrum team only (no stakeholders) |

See each linked file for how to actually run the meeting and what the concrete outcome should be.
