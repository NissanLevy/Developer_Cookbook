# Sprint Review

## Purpose

Inspect the actual increment built during the sprint, gather feedback from stakeholders, and adapt the product backlog based on that feedback. This is a **collaborative working session**, not a one-way status presentation.

## When & Duration

- Last day of the sprint, before the Retrospective.
- ~2 hours for a 2-week sprint (roughly 1 hour per week of sprint).

## Attendees

- **Whole Scrum team** — PO, Scrum Master, dev team.
- **Stakeholders** — whoever has a real interest in or influence over the product: customers, management, other teams. If no one outside the team ever attends, this meeting isn't doing its job.

## Prerequisites

- Only items that meet the team's **Definition of Done** are demoed as complete. Partially-done work is discussed, not presented as finished.
- The demo environment/data is prepared in advance — don't burn review time debugging a demo environment.

## How It Should Go

1. Scrum Master or PO briefly restates the Sprint Goal for context.
2. Team demos each completed item — ideally live/interactive, not just slides — showing real functionality, not describing it.
3. For anything **not** completed, say so plainly and explain why (this is a review of reality, not a highlight reel).
4. Stakeholders give feedback in the room, in real time — questions, reactions, new ideas.
5. PO captures new backlog items or re-prioritizations that come out of the feedback.
6. If relevant, discuss timeline, budget, or roadmap implications of progress so far.

## Outcome / Definition of Done for This Meeting

- [ ] Every completed item has been demonstrated, not just described.
- [ ] Incomplete items and the reasons are documented, not glossed over.
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback is captured somewhere durable (not just said out loud and forgotten).
- [ ] The Product Backlog is updated to reflect new information/priorities from the discussion.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Demoing incomplete or unstable work** — undermines trust in what "done" means going forward.
- **No real stakeholders present** — turns into an internal status meeting, losing the actual point of the ceremony (external feedback).
- **One-way presentation with no room for discussion** — feedback needs to actually change the backlog, not just be heard politely.
- **Treating it as a personal performance review** — the focus is the product increment, not who did or didn't finish their tickets.
