# Daily Standup (Daily Scrum)

## Purpose

A short, daily sync for the development team to align on progress toward the **Sprint Goal** and surface blockers early — not a status report to management.

## When & Duration

- Same time, same place, every working day of the sprint.
- Strictly **15 minutes**. If discussion needs longer, take it offline immediately after (see below).

## Attendees

- **Development team** — required, this is their meeting.
- **Scrum Master** — facilitates if needed, removes blockers raised.
- **Product Owner** — optional attendee, listens, doesn't run it.

## How It Should Go

Two common formats — pick one and stay consistent:

**Format A — Three questions**, one pass per person:
1. What did I complete since the last standup?
2. What am I working on until the next standup?
3. Is anything blocking me?

**Format B — Walk the board** (often scales better for larger teams): go ticket by ticket, right to left on the board, starting from "closest to done," and have whoever's working on each item give a quick update.

Either way:
- Stay focused on progress toward the **Sprint Goal**, not a generic activity log.
- If a blocker or a design discussion comes up, **name it and move on** — don't solve it in the standup. Whoever's blocked stays behind afterward with whoever can help.
- Everyone stands (or otherwise keeps it deliberately brief) to reinforce the time-box.
- If managers/stakeholders don't attend standup but need visibility, send the [Daily Update Email](DailyUpdateEmailTemplate.md) right after.

## Outcome / Definition of Done for This Meeting

- [ ] Every team member's current focus is visible to the rest of the team.
- [ ] Every blocker raised has an owner assigned to help resolve it, before the meeting ends.
- [ ] The board reflects reality — if someone's status changed, the ticket moved.
- [ ] The meeting ended within 15 minutes.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Turning into status reporting to the Scrum Master** — standup is peer-to-peer coordination, not a manager check-in.
- **Problem-solving in the room** — a 2-person technical debate about a blocker turns a 15-minute meeting into 40 minutes for everyone else. Take it offline.
- **Going through the motions with no board updates** — if standup doesn't change what's visible on the board, it isn't doing its job.
- **Skipping it on Planning/Review/Retro days** — keep it, just make it faster; the sprint goal check-in still matters those days.
